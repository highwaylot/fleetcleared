Are you ready for your safety audit?

Short answer

Answer ten yes-or-no questions based on the 16 violations that fail a new entrant safety audit automatically. Every “no” or “not sure” is something to fix or confirm before the auditor looks.

Updated September 24, 2026 · Waiting for review by a listed consultant

General information, not legal advice. Rules change: check the linked sources or a qualified consultant before acting. How to use our guides

Answer ten questions

Do you or any of your drivers drive a vehicle that requires a CDL?
1Are all your CDL drivers, including you if you drive, in a random drug and alcohol testing pool?
2Do you have a written drug and alcohol policy, and did every CDL driver pass a pre-employment drug test before driving?
3Does every truck and trailer have an annual inspection report dated within the last 12 months?
4Does every driver keep hours-of-service logs (ELD or paper), unless a written exemption applies?
5Do you have a current DOT medical card on file for every driver?
6Is your liability insurance at or above the federal minimum for what you haul or the passengers you carry?
7When a driver reports a defect that makes a truck unsafe, is it always repaired before the truck runs again?
8If a truck has been put out of service at an inspection, was it repaired before it moved again?
9Have you checked every driver's license and driving record, so you know each one is valid for the vehicle and not suspended or disqualified?
10Is any driver who failed or refused a drug or alcohol test kept off the road until they finish the return-to-duty process?

A self-check, not an audit result. It covers only the 16 automatic-fail rules, and answering yes to everything doesn't mean you'll pass. Nothing you answer is saved or sent.

How the check works

Each question covers one or more of the 16 automatic-fail rules. Questions about drug and alcohol testing and CDLs only apply if you or your drivers need a CDL. A "yes" on every question doesn't guarantee a pass: auditors look at more than these 16 rules.

After the check

Start with any "no" answers, because each one can fail the audit on its own. Then confirm the "not sure" answers by pulling the records. The audit guide lists what to have ready.
